Chandy's Story

With its humble beginnings as a bus operator, Chandys Group of Companies has expanded far and wide with multiple resorts all over Kerala. The company aims to provide a place for visitors to enjoy their stay in the indigenous surroundings to embrace nature in its rustic form. The properties are set in serene environments preserving the ecology of the land and leaving the natural beauty of the flora and fauna intact. The entire setting of the resorts are meant to evoke an earthy feeling of love of nature, where visitors can enjoy living in the warm, caressing lap of nature while enjoying the comforts of modern amenities.

Chandys Hotels & Resorts

Chandys Group is presently headed by Rahul Tom, the conscientious son of a visionary father. The group was founded by Mr. Tom Thomas in the year 1973, and who is now the chairman.

Chandys Group has always upheld its values of humanity and protecting nature while providing maximum value to its customers. The focus is on delivering beautiful experiences and activities to the customers, so when they go back home, they go back with refreshing memories of the place. Our aim is to provide them with something they can take back, it might not be tangible always, but it will always stay fresh in their memories.

The group has been making one punctilious leap after one another, with each of these resorts located at the different key locations in Kerala. At each of these locations you can hear, feel and experience the true and raw beauty of nature whispering mystical stories. You can tune out all the hustle and bustle of a busy life, and easily embrace these stories like the most precious stories you've ever collected.
